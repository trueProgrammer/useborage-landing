Start in the conversation

Your request already exists.
Do not type it again.

If the project began in ChatGPT, Claude, or another compatible assistant, use the Borage plugin or MCP connection to structure it there.

Keep the context

The conversation is useful source material

Your assistant may already contain the background, uploaded files, alternatives you considered, preferred direction, constraints, and desired deadline. Leaving that context to complete a blank form creates friction and introduces another imperfect summary.

Borage can use the connected conversation to draft the project brief, then ask only for what remains unclear.

Keep control

Convenience is not silent consent

You see the exact revision before sourcing begins. Material changes create a visible new decision. Contracts, identity checks, or payment steps may open securely when needed, but the project does not become a black box simply because it began in chat.

Your assistant can help review the brief and the result; you remain the person who approves consequential actions.

Reduce the interface

One request should feel like one request

The goal is closer to a one-click purchase than a freelance hiring campaign: express the result, confirm the complete offer, and receive the delivery. Complex work still needs care underneath. It should not require the buyer to personally operate every part of that machinery.

Choosing a service

Start rough.
Get specific later.

01What if my project does not match a page exactly?

Describe the result in your own words. Service pages are useful starting points, not a demand that you diagnose the profession or production method yourself.

02Do I have to select the professional?

No. Borage determines the capabilities required, assesses fit and availability, and returns one complete offer. You approve the terms, not a shortlist.

03Can Borage coordinate more than one specialist?

Yes, when the agreed outcome needs several capabilities. Borage can assemble and operate that delivery path while keeping one project context and one set of acceptance criteria.

04What happens before I commit?

You receive the scope, total price, target date, required inputs, included revisions, assumptions, and acceptance criteria. There is no obligation to proceed simply because you described the request.
