Privacy
notice.

Effective August 25, 2026

The project-description tool on the public homepage is an interactive preview. Text entered there is processed in your browser and is not submitted to Borage by that page.

1. Scope

This notice explains how Borage, the operator of useborage.com (“Borage,” “we,” “us”), handles personal information when you visit the website, contact us, create or receive a project, work as a professional, or use a connected Borage service.

A specific project offer, professional agreement, or connected platform may provide additional privacy information for that activity. If another organization gives you access to Borage, that organization may separately control information it supplies or requests.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Identity and business contact details, such as name, work email, company, role, and account credentials.
  • Project information, including requests, briefs, files, links, budgets, deadlines, feedback, approvals, and communications.
  • Professional information, such as capabilities, work samples, availability, rates, tax or payment details, and delivery history.
  • Support, security, and legal communications you send to us.

Information generated through use

  • Project status, revision, approval, delivery, and payment records.
  • Connection and device information, such as IP address, browser type, timestamps, referring page, and server logs.
  • Records of actions taken through a connected assistant or integration, including the project revision shown and the approval action recorded.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information, regulated health information, payment-card numbers, or confidential third-party material unless Borage has expressly confirmed an appropriate method for that project.

3. How we use information

  • To create, revise, quote, source, contract, manage, review, and close projects.
  • To match a project with relevant professionals and confirm availability and terms.
  • To operate accounts, integrations, approvals, payments, support, and service communications.
  • To secure the service, prevent misuse, investigate incidents, and maintain reliable records.
  • To improve Borage workflows using aggregated or de-identified analysis where practical.
  • To comply with legal obligations and establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

We do not sell personal information for money. We do not use information from the public homepage preview because that page does not send the entered text to us.

4. When we share information

We share only what is reasonably needed for the purpose:

  • Selected professionals and suppliers. Relevant brief, files, context, and contact or decision information needed to quote or deliver the project.
  • Service providers. Hosting, security, communications, document, payment, analytics, and support providers working for Borage under appropriate terms.
  • Your organization. Authorized account administrators, project participants, and review owners.
  • Business or legal events. Advisers, authorities, or a successor where required for law, safety, financing, restructuring, or a business transfer.

A connected assistant is operated by its own provider. Information you give that assistant is also subject to the provider’s terms and privacy notice. Borage receives only the information and actions passed through the connection.

5. Cookies and site measurement

The public landing site does not currently set nonessential advertising or analytics cookies. The hosting provider may process request logs needed to deliver and protect the site. If Borage adds analytics, advertising, or other nonessential tracking, this notice and any required controls will be updated before that tracking is enabled.

6. Retention and security

We keep information for as long as needed to deliver the service, maintain project and financial records, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations, and protect the service. Retention depends on the record type, the project agreement, and applicable law. We delete or de-identify information when it is no longer reasonably needed.

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed for the information and risk. No service can promise absolute security. Tell us promptly if you believe an account, file, or integration has been compromised.

7. Your choices and privacy rights

You may ask to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of personal information, or object to or limit certain uses. Rights vary by location and may be subject to exceptions. We may need to verify your identity and authority before acting.

Where applicable, you may appeal a denied privacy request or use an authorized agent. Borage will not discriminate against you for exercising an applicable privacy right. Because the public site does not currently sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, there is no such opt-out to process on this site.

8. Children

Borage is a business service and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the service.

9. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when the service, law, or handling practices change. We will post the revised notice here and change the effective date. Material changes may also be communicated through the service or by email where appropriate.

10. Contact

Questions and privacy requests can be sent to privacy@useborage.com. Include the email address associated with your account or project and enough detail for us to identify the request.
