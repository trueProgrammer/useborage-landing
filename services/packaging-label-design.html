Packaging questions

Before artwork
goes to press.

01Can you start without a dieline?

Concept work can sometimes begin from confirmed dimensions, but final artwork requires an approved dieline from the printer or packaging supplier.

02Does Borage write or approve regulatory copy?

Only if qualified regulatory or copy support is explicitly included. The client remains responsible for approving claims, ingredients, warnings, and other legally required content.

03Can you create several sizes or flavors?

Yes. Each size, flavor, language, or market version is listed as a named deliverable so the extension work and review load are clear.

04Is a printer proof included?

Only when stated in the offer. Borage can coordinate proof feedback, but physical print, color matching, and production approval remain with the printer and client unless separately agreed.
