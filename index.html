A few practical answers

Useful things
to know.

Clear before you begin. Clear while the work is moving.

01What kinds of projects can I bring?

Start with bounded specialist work: brand and production design, presentations, focused research, and digital assets. Describe the outcome first and Borage will make any missing requirements visible.

02Do I have to compare professionals?

No. Borage handles sourcing and fit checks. You receive one professional-confirmed offer with the relevant evidence, exact scope, price, date, and terms.

03When will I see the price and terms?

Before you commit. The offer shows the total price, delivery date, included revisions, acceptance criteria, assumptions, and anything still required from you.

04What happens if the scope changes?

There are no silent scope changes. Material changes are documented and presented for approval before they become part of the project.
