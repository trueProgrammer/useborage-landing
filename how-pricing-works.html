What shapes the price

The result determines
the delivery required.

Two projects with similar labels can require very different work. Borage prices the real brief after the important variables are visible.

01Output

Deliverables and complexity

Quantity, format, technical detail, variants, duration, resolution, editability, and intended use affect the capabilities and production time required.

  • Number and type of files
  • Level of original creation
  • Production-ready requirements
02Starting point

Clean source files and decided content reduce discovery. Missing copy, conflicting references, incomplete CAD, or unresolved direction may require additional work before production can begin.

  • Source quality
  • Decisions already made
  • Assumptions to resolve
03Delivery conditions

Deadline, number of stakeholders, approval sequence, included revisions, specialist availability, and technical checking all shape the delivery plan.

  • Target date and milestones
  • Review rounds and owners
  • External production constraints

Rate versus real cost

The cheapest hour is not always
the cheapest finished project.

A contractor rate is easy to compare because it is a small number. It does not tell you how many hours the work will consume, how much management it will need, or whether the delivered files will be usable.

Pricing questions

Before you
approve the offer.

01Is describing a project a commitment to buy?

No. Borage uses the request to clarify and prepare the delivery offer. You can review the complete terms before deciding whether to proceed.

02Why is there no universal price list?

Output count, source quality, originality, technical requirements, deadlines, revisions, and risk vary materially. A single headline price would either hide assumptions or describe a standardized product that many real projects are not.

03What if I change the scope after approval?

A material change is documented with its effect on price, timing, deliverables, or criteria. It becomes part of the project only after you approve the revised terms.

04Does the offer include project management?

Yes. Borage's role in scoping, sourcing, routine coordination, review handling, delivery checking, and included revisions is part of the managed project path described in the offer.
