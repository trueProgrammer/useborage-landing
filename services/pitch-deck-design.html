Pitch deck questions

Before the
first slide.

01Can Borage help if the story is not finished?

Yes, when narrative development is included in scope. Borage separates open business decisions from presentation work and identifies which claims or facts still need your approval.

02Does this include financial modeling or investment advice?

Not by default. The project can present approved data and supplied projections, but underlying model creation, financial verification, legal review, and investment advice require separate qualified scope.

03Will I receive an editable deck?

Yes when the native file is listed as a deliverable. The offer identifies the software, final formats, and any elements that cannot remain fully editable.

04How many revisions are included?

The exact number and purpose of review rounds appear in the offer. Material changes to the business story or scope are treated separately from corrections against the approved direction.
