Professional questions

Before you
join.

01Is Borage a public freelancer marketplace?

No. Opportunities are sent privately based on project fit. There is no public bid wall or requirement to publish a discounted offer.

02Do I have to accept an invitation?

No. You can decline. Nothing is agreed until you confirm availability and accept the exact project terms.

03Can I clarify the scope before quoting?

Yes. Open questions and assumptions should be resolved through Borage before terms are confirmed. The goal is a responsible quote, not a guess.

04What if the client asks for additional work?

A material addition is documented as a scope change with fee and timing impact. It proceeds only after the revised terms are agreed.
