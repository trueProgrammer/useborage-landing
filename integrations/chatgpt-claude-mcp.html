Connection questions

Before you
connect.

01Is this limited to ChatGPT or Claude?

No. The connection is designed for assistants that support compatible MCP connections. Availability and setup differ by host platform.

02Does the assistant start sourcing automatically?

No. Borage shows the exact current brief and requires explicit approval before the sourcing stage begins.

03Can the assistant change an approved project?

A requested material change should create a visible new revision and impact review; it should not silently replace agreed scope.

04Does every project action happen inside chat?

No. Briefing, status, review, and approval can use the connected conversation where supported. A contract, identity check, payment, sensitive file exchange, or complex comparison may open Borage securely. The exact surface depends on the stage and host platform.

05Can my assistant approve the final result for me?

Your assistant can help inspect and summarize the delivery, but it should not silently accept consequential work. You see the result and make the final acceptance decision.
