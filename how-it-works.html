Practical answers

Before you
hand it over.

01Do I choose the professional?

No. Borage handles sourcing and fit checks. You receive one confirmed offer with the relevant evidence and exact delivery terms.

02What do I approve before work starts?

The deliverables, total price, target date, your required inputs, included revisions, acceptance criteria, and known assumptions.

03Can one project require several specialists?

Yes. Borage can assemble and coordinate more than one capability when the outcome requires it. You still receive one managed delivery path.

04Does Borage guarantee that I will like every creative choice?

Borage commits to the objective criteria and agreed direction in the offer. Creative preference is handled through the included review and revision rounds, not an unlimited satisfaction promise.
