3D production questions

Before the
render starts.

01Do I need an existing 3D model?

No, but accurate dimensions and reference material are needed to build one. The offer will separate modeling from the rendering or animation work.

02Can the workflow combine 3D and generative video?

Yes, when appropriate. A 3D block-out or rendered reference can lock product shape and camera direction before a generative video pass. The method and acceptable product fidelity are agreed before production.

03Are source scene files included?

Only when listed in the offer. Final rendered assets and source 3D scenes have different handoff, licensing, and dependency requirements.

04What makes a revision expensive?

Changes to approved geometry, camera, lighting, materials, timing, or shot count can require earlier stages to be reopened. Approval gates make that impact visible before final rendering.
