Managed delivery vs freelance marketplaces

Do you need a person—
or the work done?

Freelance marketplaces are useful for discovering and hiring independent professionals. Managed project delivery is for buyers who would rather approve a complete outcome and have the sourcing, coordination, review, and checking operated for them.

What stays with the buyer

The platforms may look similar.
The responsibility is different.

Both models can use independent professionals and digital workflows. The key question is who is responsible for turning a request into a completed, accepted project.

Typical freelance marketplace

The platform helps you hire.

You generally search or post, compare professionals or predefined services, choose who to work with, agree the brief and commercial terms, give direction, answer delivery questions, manage milestones, request revisions, and decide whether the files meet your needs.

  • 01You select and contract the professional
  • 02You provide the working project management
  • 03Quality depends partly on how you run the engagement
Borage managed delivery

You approve the project, not the profile.

You describe the intended result. Borage scopes the work, determines the required capability, sources and confirms the professional, reconciles the terms into one offer, manages delivery, answers routine questions from the agreed context, coordinates revisions, and checks the result.

  • 01Borage selects for the defined outcome
  • 02Routine delivery work stays with Borage
  • 03You approve the offer, exceptions, and final result

The real economic comparison

Count management time
as part of project cost.

The professional invoice is visible. The buyer's internal time is not, even when it is the most expensive input.

Before hiring

Diagnosis and selection take work

To hire well, the buyer needs to know which discipline is appropriate, what evidence matters, how to compare portfolios, and which quotation assumptions are missing. Large supply creates options, but evaluating those options is itself a project.

If you already have that knowledge, marketplaces make it possible to use it directly. If you do not, the learning curve belongs to you.

During delivery

Small questions fragment expensive attention

A short answer can still interrupt a senior person's day. Repeated requests for files, clarification, stakeholder feedback, or production decisions make the buyer the routing layer for the project. This is rarely visible in the original rate comparison.

Managed delivery uses one approved context and escalates decisions that materially change the result, price, date, or scope.

At handoff

Acceptance needs more than a download link

Someone must verify that every promised file exists, the right versions were used, outputs open correctly, named elements remain editable, and production requirements were followed. In a self-managed engagement, that checker is usually the buyer.

Borage places the pre-delivery check inside the service and presents the result for acceptance.

Decision rule

Use a freelance marketplace when you want to choose and manage a professional. Use managed project delivery when you want to approve a bounded result and keep routine sourcing and project operation off your desk.

A chat-native difference

to begin another workflow.

Marketplaces typically begin with a search, category, job post, or service listing. Borage can begin inside a compatible assistant through a plugin or MCP connection.

Conversation as input

Your existing context becomes the draft

When the project has already been discussed in ChatGPT, Claude, or another assistant, Borage can structure the current messages and files into a visible brief. The buyer answers only what is missing rather than copying the conversation into a separate post.

Assistant as reviewer

Keep the tool you already use

The assistant can help check the brief for omissions, explain the offer, or compare the delivered result with the agreed criteria. Borage holds the project state and manages the professional; the assistant remains your interface and thinking partner.

Explicit control

Low friction does not remove consent

Consequential steps require approval. Contracts, identity, or payment may use a secure Borage surface when needed. The aim is a simpler doorway and fewer repeated explanations—not a hidden project or automatic commitment.

Choosing the model

Neither model
is always right.

01When is a freelance marketplace the better choice?

Choose a marketplace when you want broad choice, already know the required discipline, prefer direct contractor control, or are comfortable owning scope, coordination, review, and acceptance.

02Does Borage hide the professional?

Borage manages sourcing and the delivery relationship so you do not need to compare profiles. The offer provides the relevant evidence and terms needed to approve the project without turning selection into your job.

03Can managed delivery use several specialists?

Yes. When the outcome requires multiple capabilities, Borage can assemble and coordinate them under one project context and one acceptance path.

04Is Borage an employment or staffing service?

No. Borage is for defined project outcomes. If you need ongoing capacity, a long-term embedded contributor, or an employee, a staffing or hiring platform may be more appropriate.
