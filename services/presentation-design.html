Presentation questions

Before we
build the deck.

01Can Borage help if the content is not finished?

Yes, if content shaping is included in the scope. We first separate story work from design work and identify which facts or approvals still need your team.

02Can you work in our existing template?

Yes. Supply the template, fonts, brand guidelines, and any required slide library. Template cleanup or new master layouts can be included explicitly.

03Will charts and numbers be checked?

We can check that displayed figures match the approved source material. This is not an audit of the underlying business or financial data.

04Who owns the editable files?

The project offer states the usage and transfer terms. Final working files are included when listed as a deliverable.
