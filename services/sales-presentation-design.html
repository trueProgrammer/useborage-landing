Sales deck questions

Before we
shape the story.

01Can Borage work from call transcripts?

Yes. Transcripts can be useful source material for questions, language, objections, and proof. The scope determines whether synthesis and message development are included.

02Can the deck include modular slides for different buyers?

Yes, when modular sections are part of the agreed deliverables. The brief should define the core narrative and which audience or sales-stage variations matter.

03Can you use our current template?

Yes. Provide the native template, brand rules, fonts, and slide library. Cleanup or expansion of the template should be listed explicitly if required.

04Will Borage verify our product claims?

Borage can check that slides match approved source material. It does not independently certify product, legal, financial, or customer claims unless a separate qualified review is included.
