Rendering questions

Before the
first frame.

01Do I need a finished 3D model?

No, but the available inputs affect scope. CAD cleanup, modeling from drawings, sculpting, retopology, or recreation from photography must be identified and priced explicitly.

02Can one project include product variants?

Yes. The offer should list the geometry, color, label, finish, and packaging differences for each variant and map them to the required views.

03Will I receive the 3D source scene?

Only when it is listed as a deliverable with compatible software and usage terms. Final still images do not automatically imply transfer of every working asset or third-party license.

04Can Borage guarantee exact real-world color?

Digital rendering can target approved references, but perceived color varies across lighting, material, display, print, and calibration. Critical color workflows require agreed references and appropriate physical or color-managed validation.
